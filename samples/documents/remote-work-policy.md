# Northwind Energy Labs — Remote Work Policy (synthetic)

> Synthetic demonstration document for NEXUS AI. Northwind Energy Labs is a fictional company.

## Eligibility

Employees who have completed their 90-day onboarding period may work remotely up to three days per week.
Field technicians and laboratory staff are excluded because their work requires on-site equipment.

## Core hours

All remote employees must be reachable between 10:00 and 15:00 in their local time zone. Meetings that span
more than two time zones should be scheduled inside the overlapping core hours.

## Equipment and stipend

The company provides a laptop and a 27-inch monitor. Remote employees receive a one-time home office stipend of
600 US dollars and a monthly internet allowance of 45 US dollars.

## Security requirements

Company data may only be accessed through the corporate VPN. Public Wi-Fi networks must not be used without the
VPN. Lost or stolen devices must be reported to the security team within 4 hours.

## Review

This policy is reviewed every 12 months by the People Operations team. The current version took effect on
1 March 2025.
